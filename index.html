Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ's

Everything you need to know about rates, minimums, availability, and what to expect when you book studio time at Bay Eight.

What makes Bay Eight Recording Studio in Miami stand out?

Twenty-five years of work, RIAA Platinum certification, and a 4.9 rating from more than 790 Google reviews. Artists like Nas, Ed Sheeran, J Balvin, Diplo, Tainy, and Swae Lee have recorded in these rooms.

How much does studio time cost?

The SSL Suite is $135/hr, the Douglas Suite is $105/hr, and the Whisper Room is $85/hr. An engineer is included in every rate — use the session builder above to see your total before you book.

Is there a minimum booking?

Sessions have a 4-hour minimum, or a 3-hour minimum if you start before 2pm.

What hours are you open?

24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call or text 305-705-2405 any time and we'll get back to you within 15 minutes.

Is an engineer included, and can I bring my own?

A house engineer is included with every session. You can add a producer (+$50/hr), a senior engineer alongside the house engineer (+$30/hr), or a collaborative songwriter (+$50/hr). If you don't have someone in mind, we'll match you with an engineer who knows your genre.

What else comes with a session?

Free monitored parking, complimentary snacks and refreshments, and absolute privacy in the studio. We're at 15421 W Dixie Hwy, Bay 8, Miami, FL 33162.
